370|||Why does the ABYC standard matter?
371|||The ABYC (American Boat and Yacht Council) is a US-based organisation that publishes widely accepted safety standards for boat electrical systems. Cable cross-section, fuse and circuit-breaker protection, grounding and battery connections are defined in these standards. Systems that are not installed correctly carry a risk of fire and voltage loss.
372|||When should a battery bank be replaced?
373|||When its capacity drops noticeably, when it can no longer hold a charge, or when its voltage falls quickly under load. Lifespan depends on the battery type (lead-acid, AGM, lithium), how heavily it is used and the charging system. Before deciding to replace it, the capacity and the charging system are checked.
374|||How often should a generator be serviced?
375|||At the running-hour interval in the manufacturer's manual, and in any case at least once a year. The main items are oil and filter changes, the cooling system and a check of the sea-water pump impeller.
376|||Why does the battery on my boat drain quickly?
377|||Common causes are an ageing battery, an inadequate charging system (alternator or charger), leakage current and loads that stay on all the time. The capacity, charging voltage and circuits are checked.

405|||How do I book boat mechanical maintenance in Adana?

407|||Why does shaft alignment matter?
408|||If the shaft and the engine or gearbox axis are misaligned, you get vibration, noise and early wear on the bearing, stuffing box and coupling parts, which leads to more expensive repairs over time. Alignment should be checked when the engine mounts have been changed, when the boat has been hauled out and relaunched, or when you notice vibration in the shaft.
409|||How often should seacocks be serviced?
410|||At least once a year, preferably at the start of the season, by opening and closing them to check. Valves that are not moved for a long time can seize. If there is corrosion, a leak or stiff turning, they should be replaced together with the connected hose and clamps. Because this is directly linked to the risk of taking on water, the pre-season check should not be skipped.
411|||Is a bilge pump failure an emergency?
412|||If the boat is not taking on water, it may not be an emergency, but it should be fixed without delay. If it is taking on water, the situation is an emergency: first identify the source of the water and put your safety first. If the pump is not working, check the fuse, the wiring connection, the float switch and the battery, and replace the pump if necessary.
413|||How can I tell there is a problem in the steering system?
414|||Steering that becomes stiff or too loose, a feeling of play, the boat not turning equally to port and starboard, hydraulic oil leaks and noise while operating. Air in hydraulic systems can also impair the response. A check is needed before going out to sea.

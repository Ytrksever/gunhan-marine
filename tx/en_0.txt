106|||Frequently asked questions
107|||What you may want to know before getting in touch
108|||Do you only work on engines?
109|||No. Besides marine engines and propulsion systems, we offer boat mechanical and auxiliary systems, navigation electronics, marine electrical work, hull care and antifouling, pre-purchase engine and electrical surveys, and a mobile Travel Lift service.
110|||What does mobile service mean? Do I need to bring my boat?
111|||No. Our team and equipment come to wherever your boat is. Service is carried out at the marina, the boat ramp, the shipyard or your mooring on the lakeshore.
112|||How do I send a service request?
113|||You can reach us by phone, WhatsApp or email. A short note on the boat type, the engine or system and the problem speeds up the first assessment. After that, the boat is inspected on site, the scope of work is agreed and what will be done is explained to you clearly. The request form below prepares your message for you.
114|||How quickly can you come in an emergency?
115|||Urgent fault requests are answered as quickly as possible and an on-site response is planned. The fastest way to reach us is by phone or WhatsApp.
116|||Which areas do you serve?
117|||Adana (including Seyhan Dam Lake and its surroundings), Mersin and Hatay, on a mobile basis. The mobile Travel Lift service is not regional and is carried out across Turkey.
118|||Do you carry out hull or structural surveys?
119|||No. We focus on mechanical, engine and electrical systems. Hull, bottom and structural surveys are outside our scope. When you buy a used boat, we assess only the technical condition of the engine, mechanical and electrical systems.
